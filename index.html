The Dual-Market Model Explained

Why Absorbing Tickets ("EAT") & Targeted Wins Outperform

In Malaysian racing, bookmaking liquidity allows us to absorb heavy public tickets ("EAT" horses) when the market is over-supported, retaining RM 8,600 to RM 21,500 per run. When public ticket volume is thin, our employee jockeys ride to win—capturing high-odds payouts alongside RM 800 starter incentives (which offset ~90% of RM 1,250/horse club admin fees) and official stakemoney.

All 10 shares (7.5 Investor + 2.5 Trainer) hold pro-rata ownership in the 10-horse string. Monthly statements detail horse soundness, race entries, and financial reconciliations. Major bloodstock disposals or structural changes require >50% shareholder approval.
