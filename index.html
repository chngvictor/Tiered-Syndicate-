Realistic Track Economics

"Eat" Positions Drive Monthly Cash Flow; Wins Are Pure Bonus

In real Malaysian racing, expecting every horse to win monthly is unrealistic—each runner realistically wins 1 to 2 times per year (~1.25 fleet wins/month across 10 horses, with occasional standouts). Therefore, our model anchors monthly cash flow on 13 disciplined "EAT" positions (retaining RM 8,600 to RM 21,500 per run) plus RM 800 starter incentives, delivering RM 158,125/month in net profit.

Pro-Rata Distributions & Payback

04

Asset Ownership & Governance

The 10-horse string is held pro-rata (75% Investors / 25% Trainer). Monthly statements detail horse soundness, race entries, and financial reconciliations. Major bloodstock disposals or structural changes require >50% shareholder approval.
