01 / Operational Excellence

The Dual-Market Advantage & 5% Failure Rate

Traditional public syndicates suffer from a 40–50% failure rate due to external variables and unaligned riders. We have systematically eliminated those risks.

In-House Employee Jockeys

Our jockeys are direct stable employees. When instructed to absorb market ticket volume ("EAT" positioning) or launch for peak win campaigns, tactical execution is 100% disciplined with zero rider variance.

✓ Complete control over race pacing & tactical outcomes.

Direct Stable Conditioning

Personal oversight of veterinary care, shoeing, specialized joint care, and nutrition ensures horses arrive at race day 100% sound and prepared—reducing failure rates to just 5%.

✓ 95.0% institutional execution reliability.

Starter Incentive Fee Offsets

Club administrative charges of RM 1,250 per horse/month are virtually neutralized by the RM 800 starter incentive paid when horses finish outside the bottom 2 positions (~14 qualifying runs/mo).

✓ Net administrative burden reduced to near zero.
The Dual-Market Model Explained

Why Throwing Away a Race ("EAT") is Easier Than Winning

In Malaysian racing, bookmaking liquidity allows us to absorb heavy public tickets ("EAT" horses) when the market is over-supported. Conversely, when public ticket volume is thin, our employee jockeys are instructed to ride for the win. This dual-market optionality guarantees consistent cash flow regardless of traditional odds volatility.

05 / Legal & Governance

Investor Term Sheet & Governance

Transparent terms designed to protect investor capital while ensuring complete alignment of interest between syndicate partners and the trainer.

01

Offering Size & Share Valuation

Total syndicate capitalization of RM 1,118,000 – RM 1,168,000 (Midpoint: RM 1,143,000) divided into exactly 10 equal shares at RM 111,800 – RM 116,800 per share. Each share represents a 10.0% equity interest in syndicate assets and returns.

02

Trainer Alignment & Carried Interest

The Stable Trainer waives commercial training fee markups, billing strictly at RM 5,300/horse/month. In lieu of margins, the Trainer receives a 25% carried interest on net monthly operating profits. Investors retain 75% plus 100% pro-rata bloodstock ownership.

03

Reserves & Cash Distributions

Net investor distributions (projected at RM 16,275/share/month) are paid monthly or quarterly, subject to maintaining a rolling 2-month operational reserve (RM 106,000) to protect against spelling cycles.

04

Transparency & Voting Rights

Monthly operational reports cover horse soundness, race declarations, and financial reconciliations. Major bloodstock disposals or capital reallocations require a simple majority (>50%) shareholder vote.
